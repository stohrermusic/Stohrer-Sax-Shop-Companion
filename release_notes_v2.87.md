# v2.87 — Scrap Mode progress, lid question

## Pad Maker

- **Scrap Mode progress window** now has three columns: Remaining, On this scrap, and Done. It updates the moment a scrap is nested, so during the preview and the cut you can see which pads are on the piece in front of you and which are still to come. Backing out of the preview or stopping a cut moves those pads back to Remaining.
- **Frame & Cut: lid question.** "Is the lid closed?" now has two buttons, **Yes, Start Frame** and **No, Cancel**. No leaves you on the positioning dialog; close the lid and click Start Frame again.
- **Frame & Cut:** clicking Cut while the framing loop is between passes no longer stalls the run.
